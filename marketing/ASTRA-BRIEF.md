# Advert brief: Spine

You're making an advert for Spine. The screenshots in this folder are real screens from the product, with demo data for a fictional company, Acme Group. Use them as they are. You can crop, zoom, frame them in a laptop or phone, and animate between them, but don't redraw the interface or add features that aren't there.

## What Spine is

Spine is the place a company runs its AI adoption. Most companies have bought AI tools, but the good questions get lost in chat, the good ideas never get an owner, and nobody can say what AI has actually delivered. Spine fixes that, quietly: it only asks for your attention when a person is needed.

One line: **Spine makes sure good AI ideas and questions never quietly die.**

## Who it's for

- **The buyer:** the person leading AI adoption at a company of 200 to 5,000 people (Head of AI, COO, Digital Transformation, IT leadership).
- **Everyone else:** employees who want help using AI at work, and the leaders who need to see results.

## The four things it does (use these as the story)

1. **Alignment: everyone knows the rules.** The Playbook shows the company's AI priorities, which tools are approved for which data, and plain-English guidance. (`09-playbook.png`)
2. **Help: ask anything, privately.** Anyone can ask an AI question, anonymously if they like. A Champion (a colleague who's good with AI) answers in a private thread. Good answers can be shared with everyone, while the conversation stays private. (`02-help-desk.png`, `03-private-question-thread.png`, `21-phone-thread.png`, `10-discover.png`)
3. **Execution: ideas get an owner and a plan.** Ideas go through approval, form a team, get a step-by-step plan, and move to Live. Every initiative shows one plain sentence about what happens next, for example "Jamie is testing supplier cases". (`12-propose-an-idea.png`, `07-approval-decision.png`, `04-initiatives-board.png`, `05-initiative-plan.png`, `22-phone-initiative.png`)
4. **Momentum: nothing goes quiet.** One-minute check-ins, blockers that reach the right people, and Pulse, which shows anything stuck or waiting. Home tells each person exactly what needs them, starting with the most urgent. (`01-home-what-needs-you.png`, `20-phone-home.png`, `06-check-ins.png`, `08-pulse-whats-stuck.png`)

**And the payoff: proof.** Leadership sees hours saved that count only once someone outside the team has verified them, plus what's live, what's in flight and where to step in. (`11-leadership.png`)

`13-search-palette.png` shows quick search across everything. `30-member-home.png` shows what an ordinary employee sees.

## Key messages (pick 2 or 3 per advert, not all of them)

- "What needs you, first." Every screen answers what to do next.
- Private by design. Questions stay between the asker and the Champion who claims them.
- From idea to Live, with an owner, a plan and a next step.
- Results you can prove. Hours saved count only once they're verified.
- Works with what you already use. It posts to your Slack or Teams channel and exports plans to any calendar.

## Look and tone

- Calm, confident, premium. Think of a Linear or Notion launch video, not a hype reel.
- Use the product's own colours: deep navy (sidebar), warm off-white (workspace), and one cobalt blue for actions. A small green dot means "Live".
- No purple gradients, glowing orbs, robots, sparkles everywhere or sci-fi effects. Spine's whole point is that it's quiet.
- Plain English, short sentences, no jargon, no em dashes.
- The logo is the wordmark "Spine" beside three offset rounded blue bars. Take it from the top-left of any desktop screenshot.

## Hard rules (so the advert is truthful)

- Don't invent customers, logos, testimonials, statistics, prices, awards or security certifications. The numbers in the screenshots (for example "94 verified hours saved") belong to the fictional demo company. If you show them, show them inside the product, never as a claim about real results.
- Only claim features that are in the screenshots or this brief. In particular, Spine **doesn't** chat with you in Slack or Teams, read your messages or calendar, or create channels automatically. It posts updates to a channel you choose and exports plans as calendar files.
- AI in Spine drafts and suggests (for example a first answer or a first plan). People decide. Don't suggest the AI runs things on its own.
- Acme Group is fictional. Keep it that way.

## What I'd like back

1. A 30-second video advert: a script with voiceover, plus a shot-by-shot storyboard naming which screenshot each shot uses.
2. A 15-second cut-down for social.
3. Three static ads (one square, one landscape, one portrait/story), each with a headline, one line of supporting text and a call to action.

If anything here is unclear, ask rather than guess.
